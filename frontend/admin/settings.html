Percentage weight of Exam. CA + Exam should equal 100.
Attendance Threshold (%)
Minimum attendance required to pass a course.
Attendance Warning (%)
Below this triggers a warning notification.
Attendance Critical (%)
Below this contributes to RED risk category.
Count Excused as Present
Whether excused absences count toward attendance percentage.
