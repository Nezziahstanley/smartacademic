How It Works

From raw data to real action in 6 steps.

1

Academic data

Attendance, CA scores, exams, results are recorded by lecturers and admins.

2

Compute GPA/CGPA

The system calculates totals, grades, grade points, and semester performance.

3

Risk Engine

Weighted scoring evaluates attendance, CA, exam, failed courses, and GPA decline.

4

Early Warning

Students are classified GREEN, YELLOW, ORANGE, or RED — thresholds are configurable.

5

Intervention

Staff are alerted. Counselling, tutorials, meetings, or study plans are assigned.

6

Monitor & Report

Progress is tracked until the student recovers. Reports document everything.
